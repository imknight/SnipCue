# Privacy Policy for SnipCue

Updated 23 November 2026

## Data stored on this computer

SnipCue saves your product names, questions, answer text, optional labels, and feed settings in this Chrome profile. It remembers your product choice for the side panel. SnipCue has no account, developer-operated server, or analytics.

## Web pages and the clipboard

Chrome passes selected text to SnipCue only when you choose **Save as question in SnipCue**. SnipCue saves that text in your local library. If it needs you to choose or create a product, it keeps the selection temporarily in Chrome session storage. SnipCue does not use page addresses or browsing history.

SnipCue writes an answer, AI prompt, or correction message to the clipboard when you choose Copy. You decide where to paste it.

## Optional product feeds

SnipCue fetches only the HTTPS feed URLs you enter. It checks a URL when you save it, when you open the side panel or switch to its product subject to a five-minute limit, or when you select Refresh. The host receives the requested URL and ordinary network metadata such as your IP address. SnipCue attaches no cookies, account credentials, page addresses, or library records to these requests.

The downloaded answers are stored in your local library. Answers in a public repository are public, and anyone with a secret gist link can read it. Private repositories are unsupported.

## AI replies and backups

The AI prompt contains the selected product name and its question titles, with open questions first. Your chat provider handles the text you paste into it under its own terms. SnipCue validates and previews pasted replies locally and saves your selections to the library.

You can download a backup as JSON and decide where to store or share it. A backup includes your feed URLs, including any secret gist links. SnipCue does not sell your data.

## Retention and deletion

Deleting an answer, question, or product removes that record from your local library. Deleting a product also removes its questions and answers. Removing the extension or the Chrome profile can remove the whole library. Export a backup if you want to keep a copy.
